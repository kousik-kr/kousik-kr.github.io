Teaching

My teaching experience at IIT Ropar is centered on core Computer Science and Engineering courses, with hands-on laboratory mentoring, assignment evaluation, project guidance, and student support.

Teaching Statement

Build foundations through implementation

I teach technical material by connecting formal concepts to working systems. In database systems, software engineering, and algorithms, students learn best when they can see the design tradeoffs, implement the idea, test the behavior, and explain why the method works.
