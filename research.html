Research Interests

My research focuses on developing efficient algorithms and systems for managing and analyzing spatio-temporal data, with applications in navigation, logistics, and large-scale data processing.

Spatio-temporal Databases

Developing advanced data structures and query optimization techniques for managing time-dependent spatial data. Focus on scalable solutions for large-scale systems with dynamic geospatial information.

Navigation Systems

Designing efficient algorithms for route planning in complex transportation networks. Research includes real-time path optimization and handling traffic dynamics in urban environments.

Graph Data Structures

Creating scalable graph libraries for fast information retrieval and processing. Developing specialized data structures optimized for spatial and temporal queries.

Parallel Algorithm Design

Implementing multi-threaded and distributed algorithms for computational efficiency. Exploring parallelization strategies for graph algorithms and path optimization problems.

Time-Dependent Road Networks

Addressing challenges in routing with time-varying edge weights. Developing solutions for constrained path problems in dynamic transportation networks.

Vehicle Logistics Optimization

Streamlining pickup and delivery operations considering real-world constraints. Optimizing loading/unloading costs and route efficiency in logistics systems.

Constrained Path Planning

Solving path optimization under multiple constraints including time windows, capacity limits, and resource availability in complex networks.

Scenic Route Optimization

Developing bidirectional algorithms for finding optimal routes that balance travel time with scenic value and user preferences.

Integration of Cutting-Edge Technologies

Exploring applications of machine learning, cloud computing, and distributed systems in spatial data management and navigation.

Research Impact

My research contributions span multiple domains with publications in prestigious conferences such as ACM SIGSPATIAL, WISE, DATE, and IEEE. The work focuses on practical solutions to real-world problems in navigation, logistics, and data management, with applications in smart cities, transportation systems, and location-based services.
