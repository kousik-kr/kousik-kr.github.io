Building scalable ways to reason about networks

My work connects algorithm design with database and systems questions that arise when networks are large, dynamic, and shaped by competing preferences.

Three broad areas of ongoing work

Ongoing research

Constrained and Preference-Aware Path Query Processing

Investigating efficient approaches for processing constrained and preference-aware path queries in large transportation networks, with particular interest in how route choices evolve under time-dependent conditions.

Path queriesPreferencesTransportation networks
Ongoing research

Scalable Graph Representations and Path-Closure Processing

Exploring graph representations and query-processing techniques that support efficient analysis of connectivity and path relationships in large networks, with an emphasis on scalable data management and reusable computation.

Graph dataPath relationshipsScalable systems
Ongoing research

GPU-Accelerated Graph Analytics and Query Processing

Exploring parallel and hardware-accelerated approaches for large-scale graph workloads, particularly the challenges of irregular computation, data movement, and scalable path-query execution.
