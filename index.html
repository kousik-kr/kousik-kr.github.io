About

Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology Ropar, working at the intersection of spatial and network data management, constrained route planning, and scalable graph algorithms. My current research develops a hierarchical, operator-ready loopless path-closure layer that stores reusable path records and forms query-specific results through efficient composition. This work extends my research on optimization in large time-dependent road networks and supports a broader agenda spanning database systems, high-performance graph processing, and intelligent transportation.
