About

Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Ropar with research interests in spatial data management, route planning, and scalable graph algorithms. My work focuses on developing efficient algorithms and systems for constrained optimization in large time-dependent networks and intelligent transportation systems. I aim to build scalable and intelligent graph-processing solutions by integrating algorithmic techniques, data systems, and high-performance computing approaches.
