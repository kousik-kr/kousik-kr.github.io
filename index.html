Faculty Candidate Profile

I am a Ph.D. researcher in Computer Science and Engineering at Indian Institute of Technology Ropar. My research lies at the intersection of spatial data management, route planning, large-scale graph algorithms, and intelligent transportation systems. I design scalable algorithmic and systems solutions for constrained optimization problems on large time-dependent networks.

My current faculty application profile is built around three strengths: a focused research agenda in graph and spatial data systems, hands-on teaching experience in core CSE courses, and a publication record spanning Geoinformatica, WISE, MDM, DATE, IEEE SmartWorld, and HiPC workshop venues.
