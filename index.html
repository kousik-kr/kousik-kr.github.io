About

Algorithms, systems, and useful research

I am a computer science researcher working on preference-aware routing, time-dependent road networks, and scalable graph algorithms. I completed my PhD defence in Computer Science and Engineering at IIT Ropar in September 2026, after doctoral work on constrained maximization of preferences on time-dependent road networks. My research combines algorithms and systems, with an evolving agenda in database systems, spatial and network data management, scalable computing, and graph query processing. I am interested in building research programmes that connect rigorous algorithm design with useful systems, and in teaching the foundations that help students reason about data, software, and computation. I am open to collaboration on graph algorithms, data systems, transportation networks, and related teaching or research initiatives.

A Parallelizable Algorithm for Constrained Maximization of Preferences in Large Time-Dependent Graphs

Kousik Kumar Dutta, Venkata M. V. Gunturi

GeoInformatica
Volume 30, article 19 (2026)

Journal impact factor: 2.5 (2025)

This paper studies route selection that maximizes preferences such as safety or scenic value while respecting a travel-time limit on time-dependent networks. Its recursive approach reuses intermediate computations and exposes independent work for multicore execution. Evaluation on large road networks examines route quality and runtime relative to existing methods.

A User-Configurable Navigation System for Wideness and Turn-Aware Routing

Kousik Kumar Dutta, Venkata M. V. Gunturi

27th IEEE International Conference on Mobile Data Management (MDM)
pp. 339–342 (2026)

MDM: B (ICORE2026)

This demonstration presents an interactive navigation system for long or towing vehicles. Users can adjust the balance between road width, difficult turns, and acceptable journey time, then explore alternative routes under changing road conditions. The interface combines preference switching with map-based visualization to make the safety–travel-time trade-off easier to inspect.

Interval Based Constrained Path Optimization in Time-Dependent Road Networks

Kousik Kumar Dutta, Venkata M. V. Gunturi

Web Information Systems Engineering – WISE 2025, Proceedings, Part II
Lecture Notes in Computer Science 16368, pp. 165–176 (published 2026)

WISE: B (ICORE2026)

This work extends constrained route optimization from a fixed departure time to a departure interval. It seeks a suitable route and departure time together, maximizing a user preference within a travel-time constraint. The algorithm reduces repeated computation across the interval and is evaluated against adapted single-departure approaches on real road networks.

A Fairness Conscious Cache Replacement Policy for Last Level Cache

Kousik Kumar Dutta, Prathamesh Nitin Tanksale, Shirshendu Das

Design, Automation and Test in Europe (DATE 2021)
pp. 695–700 (2021)

DATE: A (ICORE2026)

This paper studies cache replacement with fairness among competing workloads in the last-level cache. It considers how replacement decisions can balance performance and shared-resource treatment, and evaluates the policy using system-level cache experiments. The work addresses a computer architecture problem alongside the later focus on graph and data management.
