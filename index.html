About Me

I am a Ph.D. scholar at IIT Ropar specializing in spatio-temporal databases, navigation systems, graph library, and parallel algorithm design. Proficient in Java programming, I have successfully developed a scalable graph library enabling fast information retrieval. My research reflects diversity, with publications across multiple domains. I am passionate about integrating other cutting-edge technologies into my domain to contribute to the community.
